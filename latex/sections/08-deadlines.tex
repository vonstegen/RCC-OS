\section{Slippage handling}

A slipped deadline is recorded in this chapter and triggers one of
two responses:

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Scope cut}: the stage's deliverables are reduced.
        The reduction is itself a new ADR.
  \item \textbf{Resource request}: the lab recruits a contributor.
        Recorded in the next lab newsletter.
\end{itemize}

Slippage is never hidden. A deadline is updated, not silently
extended.
